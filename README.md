Brain Tumor Detection, Classification, and Localization AI

An advanced AI-powered system designed to assist medical professionals in analyzing brain MRI scans. The model detects, classifies, and localizes brain tumors while offering transparent insights through Explainable AI (XAI).

Key Features
- Tumor Detection & Classification: Automatically identifies the presence of a brain tumor and determines its specific type.
- Tumor Localization: Pinpoints the precise location of the tumor within the brain scan.
- Explainable AI (XAI): Provides transparent, interpretable insights detailing how the model reached its classification decision to support clinical trust.
